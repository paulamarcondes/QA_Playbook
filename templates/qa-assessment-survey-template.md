# QA Assessment Survey Template

Use this survey when joining a new team, starting a quality improvement initiative, or reviewing progress after a few sprints or months.

The goal is not to audit people. The goal is to understand how quality currently works and where small changes can bring the most value.

## How to use

1. Run the survey as a team discussion or anonymous form.
2. Score each item from 1 to 5.
3. Capture evidence, examples, and pain points.
4. Pick 2 or 3 improvement actions only.
5. Repeat after 6 to 12 weeks to compare progress.

## Score meaning

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Not in place |
| 2 | Inconsistent or informal |
| 3 | Defined but not reliable |
| 4 | Working well |
| 5 | Strong, measured, and continuously improved |

## Practical reminder

A maturity survey only creates value when it leads to small, visible improvements. Start with the highest-impact gaps and keep the improvement plan realistic.
